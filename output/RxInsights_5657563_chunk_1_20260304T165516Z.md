# Demonstrating overall value of PBM management

## Overall Structure
The chart visually explains how PBM (Pharmacy Benefit Management) interventions reduce net drug costs.
It starts with a high initial cost on the left and shows sequential cost reductions across multiple categories, ending with a lower managed net cost on the right.
Bars are color-coded:
Gray: Starting baseline cost
Blue: Cost reductions or savings
Final blue bar: Resulting managed net cost

## Y-Axis
Represents cost in USD, ranging from $0 to approximately $1.2B.
Values are shown in millions (M) or billions (B).

## Data Elements (Left to Right)
1. Unmanaged Net Cost*

Value: $1B
Color: Gray
Represents the baseline drug spend without PBM management.

2. Specialty AWP Discount

Value: $156.7M
Color: Blue
Indicates savings from discounts applied to specialty drugs based on Average Wholesale Price (AWP).

3. Non-Specialty AWP Discount

Value: $432.3M
Color: Blue
Represents savings from AWP discounts on non-specialty drugs.
This is the largest single savings component in the chart.

4. Specialty Rebates†

Value: $50.5M
Color: Blue
Savings from rebates negotiated for specialty medications.

5. Non-Specialty Rebates†

Value: $59.9M
Color: Blue
Savings from rebates on non-specialty medications.

6. Specialty UM **

Value: $33.4M
Color: Blue
Savings attributed to utilization management (UM) controls for specialty drugs.

7. Non-Specialty UM **

Value: $29.6M
Color: Blue
Savings from utilization management applied to non-specialty drugs.

8. Drug Savings Review*

Value: $56.1K
Color: Blue
A very small savings component, shown in thousands rather than millions.

9. Managed Net Cost

Value: $249.1M
Color: Blue
Final net cost after all PBM discounts, rebates, and utilization management strategies are applied.

## Key Relationships and Meaning
- The chart demonstrates a reduction from $1B unmanaged cost to $249.1M managed net cost.
- Total savings are achieved through:
AWP discounts (specialty and non-specialty)
Rebates (specialty and non-specialty)
Utilization management (UM)
Minor drug savings reviews
- Non-specialty AWP discounts contribute the largest portion of savings, followed by specialty AWP discounts.
- The final managed cost is roughly 25% of the original unmanaged cost, implying approximately 75% total cost reduction.

## Intended Message of the Chart
The visual is designed to demonstrate the cumulative financial value of PBM management, showing how layered cost-control mechanisms significantly reduce overall pharmacy spend.
